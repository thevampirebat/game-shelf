Add a store listing

Paste the exact product URL. You can also add from the toolbar while viewing a listing.

Prices are read on the next price check. Amazon UK uses the currency shown on the page. CeX tracks the retail selling price, not trade-in values.

Manage product

Paused products keep their history and are skipped by all scans until resumed.

Target-price alert

Leave the price empty to disable. Alerts are checked on the next successful price reading, even if the price already meets your target. Repeated checks stay quiet until that listing rises above the target and reaches it again. Global notifications must be enabled.

Add a store column

Firefox will ask for access to this exact website. Then open a listing, use Pick price on page in the toolbar popup, and save it to a product. Use the final website address after any redirects. To restore access to an imported store, enter that same website here again.

Manage stores

Use the left/right arrows to reorder store columns. Choose a colour for each store. Colours mark headings, listing cards and recent changes. Deleting a store removes its listings and their price histories; product rows and Recent changes remain. Export a backup first if you may need them again.

Currency settings

Automatic rates refresh every 12 hours using Frankfurter’s daily reference rates. Original prices remain visible; conversions are estimates, excluding bank fees, shipping and import charges.

Custom rates / override automatic rates

Optional: enter how much 1 unit of a foreign currency is worth in your preferred currency. Example only: with EUR selected, GBP=1.18 means 1 GBP equals 1.18 EUR. A custom rate stays in use until you change or remove it.

Price check schedule

Enter 1 for hourly checks or 2 for every two hours. The first check runs one interval after you save.

Check on these days

Times use your computer’s local timezone. Keep Firefox open. If a check is missed while Firefox is closed, one catch-up check runs when the extension starts, then the schedule resumes.

Edit store

Existing listings retain their currency and history. Change a listing’s currency under Manage product. To change the website or region path, add a new store column.
