1. Add a store and a product

  1. Open the exact product page in Firefox. Select the size, edition or colour you want first.
  2. Open the extension popup and click Add this store or + Add store. Choose the currency actually displayed on the website.
  3. Click Add store and read price, then allow website access. The store is added in the background. If Firefox closes the popup, reopen it; there is no need to add the store again.
  4. Check the Price found amount. Choose an existing product row or enter a new product name, category and variant. Click Track this product.

To compare stores, add another website's listing to the same product row. Keep different editions, formats and sizes in separate rows when they should not be compared.

2. Choose a price when detection needs help

  1. Open the product page and wait for its selling price to load.
  2. Open the extension popup and click Choose a different price on the page. The popup closes and a banner appears on the store page.
  3. Move over the current selling price. The outline shows what you are selecting. Click the complete amount; avoid the crossed-out old price, monthly instalment, trade-in value or another product's price.
  4. Reopen the extension popup. Confirm Price found, then click Track this product or Update tracked listing to save the selection.

Press Esc on the store page to cancel. If the site redesigns its price area, repeat these steps. Prices inside some embedded frames or special page components may not be accessible.

3. Alerts and schedules

To set a target, open a product’s ••• menu and use Target-price alert. For example, enter 10 and EUR to alert at €10 or less. Each listing alerts once when it meets the target, then rearms only after a successful reading above the target. The first successful check after saving can alert immediately if the price already qualifies. Target alerts ignore the minimum-change threshold and the price-increase switch, but respect the global notifications switch. Missing or expired conversion rates prevent foreign-currency target checks. The target retains its selected currency if you change your preferred currency. A failed price check does not reset the alert. Importing a backup rearms target alerts.

On the grid, Enable price notifications controls all alerts. Untick Include price increases to receive only price-drop alerts. Increases still appear in your history. The minimum change is measured in your preferred currency.

Change schedule offers daily, twice-daily, weekly, twice-weekly, custom weekdays or manual-only checks. Times use your computer's local timezone. Keep Firefox open for scheduled checks.

Currency settings controls automatic reference rates and optional custom rates. Converted amounts are estimates and exclude shipping, checkout taxes, bank fees and import charges.

4. Keep a local backup

  1. Open the grid and select Backups & colours.
  2. Enable automatic backups, choose daily or weekly, and set a folder name. The folder is inside your Firefox Downloads directory; it is not an absolute computer path.
  3. Save settings and grant the optional downloads permission. A backup is requested immediately. Check that the status becomes Saved.
  4. Use Back up now whenever you want another copy. Automatic backups are checked hourly while Firefox runs, so a due backup may occur up to an hour later.

Files have dated names and are never automatically deleted. You can remove older copies yourself. Backups contain your product names, store URLs, histories and settings as unencrypted JSON. They do not include Firefox passwords, cookies, browsing history or Google credentials. Keep them somewhere you trust.

Restore with Import backup in the grid. Import replaces the current watchlist, so export a copy first. Website access must be granted separately. Automatic backup is switched off after import until you deliberately enable it again.

Optional Google Drive copy

This version uses Google Drive for desktop folder sync, not a built-in Google login. The extension writes files locally and has no access to your Drive account. Only use this option if you want the selected folder's contents uploaded to Google.

  1. Create one successful local backup and locate its folder, normally Downloads/Game-Shelf-Backups.
  2. Open Google Drive for desktop preferences. Under folders from your computer, add only that backup folder and select Sync with Google Drive.
  3. Let Drive finish syncing and verify the file in Drive. The extension's Saved status confirms only the local download; it cannot confirm cloud sync.

Avoid selecting your entire Downloads folder unless you want all its contents synced. Backups are snapshots, not two-way watchlist synchronisation.

Google's Drive for desktop guide ↗

Why isn't there a direct Google sign-in?

Direct Drive integration needs a registered Google OAuth application and its own account-permission flow. Folder sync is the available option in this build: it works with the normal desktop app and keeps account credentials outside the extension.

5. Make the grid yours

Open Backups & colours, choose an accent, page background and tile / panel background colour, then save. Tile text automatically switches between light and dark to suit the tile colour. Use Reset colours to return to the default. Store-specific colours remain independent under Manage stores, where the arrows also rearrange columns.

If something goes wrong

The store asks me to verify I'm human

Open the listing normally in Firefox and complete the site's verification, then retry. The extension does not bypass verification.

A backup failed

Check the backup status and Firefox's Downloads list. Confirm downloads permission, available disk space and the destination folder. Retry with Back up now. Do not treat Saving as confirmation of a successful file.

I use an unsigned temporary add-on

Export or download a backup before restarting Firefox or removing the extension. Temporary add-ons are removed on restart; do not rely on their local storage surviving reinstallation.

6. Tracking tools

Retry failed retries the orange listings. The optional scheduled retry makes one extra attempt for read errors, without repeatedly reopening verification pages. Retry progress counts attempts.

Under Price alerts → Notification style, choose individual notifications or one summary at the end of a scan. The full summary stays under Latest scan alert summary. Both styles respect the notification switches and thresholds. A scan with no qualifying alerts stays quiet.

Notify when back in stock requires a confirmed unavailable reading followed by confirmed availability. Stores that do not report stock explicitly cannot trigger this alert.

Open a product's ••• menu for price-history charts, Pause / mark as purchased, and per-listing edition, condition or size details. Paused products keep their history and are excluded from scans until resumed.

Enter delivery costs per listing in its displayed currency. Leave blank for unknown, or enter 0 for free delivery. The grid switch compares known delivered totals; missing shipping and unavailable exchange rates are excluded. Shipping is entered manually, and taxes, import fees and vouchers are excluded. Price alerts and targets still use the item price.

The scan's remaining-time estimate adapts as checks finish. Retries and slow pages may change it. Duplicate URLs stay in their existing row; platform and edition warnings are suggestions to review, not guaranteed matching.

Privacy and website connections

Amazon and popup delivery

To switch an existing Amazon listing from a manually picked price, open the popup and choose Use automatic Amazon Buy New price, then save. Automatic mode prefers recognised Buy New purchase-box slots and falls back to the main offer. Enter delivery cost before saving: blank means unknown and zero means free. Check the currency label.

When a store needs verification

Game Shelf checks saved listings in ordinary Firefox tabs. If a store blocks a check, open its listing using the orange notice on the grid, complete any verification, then choose Retry failed. Solving a challenge can help later checks, but the store may ask again. You can turn off failed-check notifications under Alerts. No automatic CAPTCHA solving is provided.

ShopTo image prices

Some ShopTo prices are images. Choose the large current selling-price image; Game Shelf reads its matching product offer data. Ensure the store currency matches the page. Sold-out products remain unavailable during automatic scans.

Learning store price layouts

When you successfully choose a price on a page, Game Shelf can remember its layout locally and try it on other products from the same site, store region and currency. It only reuses a layout when exactly one visible price matches. Product data and individual listing selections take priority. Ambiguous or changed layouts require choosing the price again.

Under Automatic checks, use Learn store layouts from picked prices to stop learning and using shared layouts, or Forget learned store layouts to clear them. Individual listing selections remain saved. Layouts are included in your local backup.

For prices such as 59,99€, configure the store currency as EUR. Choose the current selling price rather than the crossed-out price, delivery charge or an instalment.

Stock status

Game Shelf uses explicit stock messages and product availability data. A missing price alone remains a failed read. Amazon’s See All Buying Options is labelled No featured offer: other sellers may still have stock. It does not start an out-of-stock timer or send a stock alert.

To track a listing, turn on Watch stock when you add it in the popup or grid. For a saved listing, open Manage product, change Watch stock, and save the listing details.

Choose Check stock to scan watched listings, or check just one from Manage product. Use Change stock schedule to set times and days separately from price checks, or choose Repeat every few hours and enter an interval from 1 to 23 hours. Stock scheduling starts in manual mode and needs Firefox open to run. The first confirmed stock reading establishes the baseline for return alerts.

Alerts lets you change notifications for watched listings, extend return alerts to other listings, and opt into out-of-stock alerts. Stock alerts respect the main notifications switch and the selected individual or summary style. If a site cannot confirm stock, Game Shelf keeps its last confirmed status.

Backups & colours has an Out-of-stock tiles colour. It is separate from orange failed readings and purple verification pages. Last known prices remain visible.

Middle-click scrolling and summary backups

Middle-click a blank area inside the price grid, then move the pointer up/down to scroll the page or left/right to scroll across stores. Click again or press Escape to stop. Middle-clicking a product link still opens it normally.

Your backup includes the latest scan alert summary, its date and messages. Restoring a backup now restores that summary too. Lowest-price highlighting returns after imported prices are checked again.

Keeping scan summaries

Scan alerts are saved as they arrive, in both individual and summary notification modes. The notification style changes how Firefox alerts are delivered; it no longer controls whether a local summary is kept. Scans without matching alerts keep the previous summary.

If Firefox closes during a scan, the alerts already saved remain available and are marked as an interrupted scan after restart. The latest summary is included in backups. Summaries that were never saved by an older version cannot be reconstructed automatically.

ShopTo euro prices

Set the ShopTo store currency to EUR. For a saved listing, check its Listing currency in the product menu too. With website access allowed, Game Shelf follows the site’s EUR currency link automatically on ShopTo product-page navigation, and before popup reads or scans.

Currency URL changes keep the saved product and its history. Explicit GBP listings remain in GBP. Switching is limited to two attempts per product per tab within 90 seconds; if ShopTo keeps reverting, the reader retains your previous price and reports the currency mismatch rather than storing pounds as euros.

This uses ShopTo’s actual euro price, not an exchange-rate conversion. It applies to recognised product pages; account and checkout pages are not changed.

Remove listings quickly

Each listing tile has an × in its top-right corner. Click it to remove only that listing, without a confirmation dialog or opening the product menu. Product rows and other listings stay in place.

Use Undo last in the floating notice to restore the most recent removal, including its price history, delivery cost and saved price selection. Repeat Undo last to restore several removals. Up to 100 quick removals can be undone during the current Firefox session, even after reloading the grid. Restarting Firefox, importing a backup or dismissing the undo notice clears this undo history.

If you delete a product row or its store separately, restore the store or use a backup as needed. Undo will not duplicate a listing you have already added again.

Faster scans and shared price locations

The progress tile shows Last run length after a check finishes, for example 41m 02s. It remains available after page/browser restarts and in backups. A stopped or interrupted check is marked partial.

Game Shelf reads while a product page is loading. Most stores close after the first valid price; Boox has a short bundle-settling wait. Confirmed page-not-found messages close the tab immediately and are excluded from automatic retry. Pages with no usable price have a 20-second reading budget.

Keep Learn store layouts from picked prices enabled under Automatic checks. Choosing a price teaches a reusable location when the element can be identified unambiguously. Successful checks of existing picked listings can teach the same location too, so you do not need to repick every product.

Up to five layouts are retained for each site/store region/currency. They are reused on other products from that store, ahead of generic product price metadata; explicit stock-out messages still take priority. Missing or ambiguous layouts fall back to other supported methods or ask you to pick again. Clear saved layouts with Forget learned store layouts.

The first recognised price may appear before a store finishes later updates. For sites with a delayed discount, choose the final selling-price element so the extension watches the intended amount.

Boox bundles and Smyths verification

For Boox, select the intended bundle and save its full product URL, including the variant value. Game Shelf waits for that variant to be selected, then requires its price and variant to stay stable for 1.5 seconds, with at least 2.5 seconds before accepting a price. Automatic readings can use the exact selected variant offer rather than a standard-bundle price.

On Smyths, the scan tab stays open for up to three minutes from the first detected verification challenge. Temporary read errors do not end this wait. Prices and confirmed stock results must settle for three seconds, with a ten-second minimum check to catch delayed challenges. Stop still cancels immediately. Complete any manual verification in the scan window if requested. Bring scan window to foreground under Automatic checks can help when the site requires focus. A challenge that remains unresolved is marked Verification needed.

Other stores keep their faster first-price behaviour. Confirmed missing pages still close immediately. Boox checks with a changing or mismatched edition retain the previous saved price rather than accepting an unsettled result.

Recent price and stock changes

The date picker is always visible. Choose a date to show that day directly, or use Today and All time. Confirmed Out of stock and Back in stock changes are recorded even when notifications are off. Repeated checks of the same stock state do not add duplicates, and failed or unknown readings do not count as a return to stock.

Newest first includes price and stock changes together. Price-drop sorts put stock changes after the reductions. Up to 30 entries are shown and 500 retained. Stock events are included in backups and remain after restart. Recording starts with this version; previous stock transitions cannot be reconstructed.
